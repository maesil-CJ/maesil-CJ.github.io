Projects

ManiMo

EC601 team project, Boston University, Fall 2026 (in progress)

Goal: a disaster-response mobile manipulator that grips irregular objects firmly enough not to drop them and gently enough not to crush them, and adapts its motion when people are nearby.

Sprint 1, working in Isaac Sim: I developed the ROS 2 mission node for a wheeled base with a Franka Panda arm. The robot surveys 360°, matches 2D LiDAR object clusters to camera-based color detection to find which table holds the target cube, parks squarely using a LiDAR line fit of the table face, then picks the cube and carries it to the other table, with a LiDAR emergency stop throughout.
